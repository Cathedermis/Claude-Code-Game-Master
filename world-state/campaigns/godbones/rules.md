# Godbones: House Rules

Core: d20 + stat mod (+ proficiency where it fits) vs DC (10 easy, 15 moderate, 20 hard, 25 brutal). D&D-style HP, AC, death saves.

## Marrowtongue (magic)
- Speak the true name of a thing in the dead god's tongue and it obeys. Any effect is possible; the cost scales with ambition.
- **Know the word first.** Casting needs the right words. Words come from a Lantern Tutor (safe, slow, expensive) or from guessing (INT or WIS check, DC by how obscure the word is; on a fail the word means something else).
- **Casting check:** INT (studied) or WIS (instinctive) vs DC set by the effect's scale.
- **Spell tier (severity for the Toll):**
  - 0 Cantrip-scale: light a wick, warm your hands, find a dropped key.
  - 2 Minor: shove a person, mend a bone-crack, silence a room.
  - 5 Major: hurl someone across a street, heal a mortal wound, put out a house fire.
  - 9 Grand: bend a rib, still a crowd, speak to the dead god.
  - 14 Mythic: anything a god would hesitate over.
- **Untutored:** +3 severity until a Tutor has taught you at least the basics of the word.
- **Near the bones:** a Major-or-larger working inside the Ribcage advances The Stirring by 1 (Grand or larger by 2).
- **The Toll:** after the working, roll the Toll (price roll) with severity = tier (+3 if untutored). Read the ladder. An ally who knows even one word can take half the cost.

## The Stirring (0-6)
Town-wide meter of how close Aldrumm is to waking. Rises from big magic near the bones, desecration, and Choir rites. Thresholds: 2 shared dreams, 4 bone-quake, 6 an eye opens.

## Bearer Standing
Opening reaction of townsfolk: 2d6 + standing (-3 to +3). Bearer's Ring or a known kindness to the old families raises it; a known slight lowers it.

## Marrow
Common currency of the trade alongside coin. A thumb-vial of raw marrow sells for about 5 sp; refined lamp-marrow about 2 gp.

## Carrion Rot (0-6, PC vital `rot`)
Stolen Choir power rots the thief.
- **Harvest:** when you kill a Choir disciple you may eat their last breath to take one of their powers. +1 Rot, and a CON save DC 13 or +1 more.
- **Using a stolen power:** +1 Rot per use unless the power says otherwise.
- **Thresholds:** 2 cravings · 4 visible rot, Choir can sense you within a street · 6 Hollow-sung (character lost).
- **Lowering Rot:** not yet known. Rumor says the Chapel, the Tutors, and the Hearthfather himself each have an answer, and each answer costs something.
