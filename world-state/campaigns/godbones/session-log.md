# Session Log - Godbones

*A new adventure begins...*

## World Summary
Three hundred years ago the god Aldrumm the Hearthfather was killed by the Carrion Queen and fell across the Weeping Valley. Twelve Bearer families dragged the body into place and built the town of Godbones inside the ribcage. The Chapel of the Long Wake insists he is only sleeping. Marrow yields are dropping, the town shares dreams, and the Hollow Choir has arrived.

- **Starting location:** Rib Row, market lane along the seventh rib. Midday, market day, 3rd of Harvestwane, Year 302 of the Bearing.
- **On stage:** Mother Gristle (Lantern Tutor, pickle shop), Pip Haddersley (street kid speaking Marrowtongue in his sleep), Deacon Ilsabet Vane (Chapel, secretly Hollow Choir).
- **Hook:** Pip collapses mid-street and speaks a word of the god's tongue; lamps flare, a window shatters. Vane wants to carry him to the Chapel "for care." Gristle has gone very still.
- **Threat clock:** The Hollow Choir claims Aldrumm's heart (0/4).

### Session Ended: 2026-11-25 08:49:05 UTC
Session 1, 3rd to 7th of Harvestwane, Year 302. Thane Hearthless, Aldrumm's many-times-grandson, came to Godbones following a dream, trained under Mother Gristle, and bound Deacon Ilsabet Vane (secretly Hollow Choir) to return the dream-boy Pip by supper. Reaching for the god in the town's shared dream, he failed a Grand working, set off a bone-quake that showed the Choir the heart's door, and died paying the Toll in full, letting go of Gristle's hand to spare her. The Choir took his body and opened the door a crack with his dead hand. Pip Haddersley took up the story: he prayed, escaped the Quiet Rooms through a god-softened seam, and kept his vow by waking Aldrumm through himself (Stirring 6/6). The god shut the Queen out and owed Pip a promise; Pip spent it keeping Thane: Thane's Lamp never goes out and every hearth in town speaks his name. Pip hid until Vane's bound oath broke and burned her in front of the Marrow Guild, allied with Brannoc Tull, freed Orla Coyle and fourteen Guild children from the Quiet Rooms, beat Vane to ash at the heart's door, and with Gristle sealed the door forever with the Heartstone.
