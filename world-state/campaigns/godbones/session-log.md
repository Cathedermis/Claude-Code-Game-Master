# Session Log - Godbones

*A new adventure begins...*

## World Summary
Three hundred years ago the god Aldrumm the Hearthfather was killed by the Carrion Queen and fell across the Weeping Valley. Twelve Bearer families dragged the body into place and built the town of Godbones inside the ribcage. The Chapel of the Long Wake insists he is only sleeping. Marrow yields are dropping, the town shares dreams, and the Hollow Choir has arrived.

- **Starting location:** Rib Row, market lane along the seventh rib. Midday, market day, 3rd of Harvestwane, Year 302 of the Bearing.
- **On stage:** Mother Gristle (Lantern Tutor, pickle shop), Pip Haddersley (street kid speaking Marrowtongue in his sleep), Deacon Ilsabet Vane (Chapel, secretly Hollow Choir).
- **Hook:** Pip collapses mid-street and speaks a word of the god's tongue; lamps flare, a window shatters. Vane wants to carry him to the Chapel "for care." Gristle has gone very still.
- **Threat clock:** The Hollow Choir claims Aldrumm's heart (0/4).
